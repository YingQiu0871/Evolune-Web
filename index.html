An open-source Android & Wear OS companion for personal health routines

Medication planning,
with room to breathe.

Evolune helps you bring structure to everyday medication routines: create personal plans, record doses, review supported health-related calculations, and keep your own information available when you move to a new device.

Designed around local storage, clear permission choices, and data actions that you start and control yourself.

A personal tool, built to stay out of the way

Evolune keeps the everyday experience close to you and your device. It turns recurring medication routines and personal records into something easier to review, while leaving important choices—what to record, what to connect, and what to back up—with the person who knows the context best: you.

A closer look

Clear, focused tools for
everyday continuity.

01

Medication planning

Create and manage personal medication schedules, dose plans, reminders, and dose records in one place, so the routine you rely on is easier to understand and revisit.

02

Health Connect

When you enable the feature and give permission, Evolune can read body-weight data from Android Health Connect to support the health-related calculations the app offers.

READ_WEIGHT only · foreground use · user-controlled
03

Encrypted backups

Before a backup leaves your device, it is encrypted locally with a passphrase. You keep control of the passphrase required to restore that backup later.

AES-256-GCM · PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256
04

Wear OS companion

View relevant medication information and record supported dose actions from a connected Wear OS device, keeping a small part of the routine close at hand.

Optional when you need it

Why Evolune asks for Google Drive access

Google Drive is an optional part of Evolune, used only when you deliberately choose to back up or restore your app data. Evolune stores those backups in Google’s private application-specific appDataFolder; it does not browse, read, or change unrelated files in your Drive.

Learn more in our Privacy Policy ↗

Open by design

Open source,
built with care.

Evolune is an open-source Android and Wear OS project maintained by Yingqiu. The source is available for people who want to understand how the app works, and the application does not operate a developer backend that stores users’ medication records or copies of their Google Drive backups.
