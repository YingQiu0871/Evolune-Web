A personal tool, not a portal

Evolune keeps its center of gravity on the device. It is designed to make recurring medication routines and personal records easier to understand, while leaving important decisions and data movement in the user’s hands.

What it does

Simple surfaces for
everyday continuity.

01

Medication planning

Create and manage personal medication schedules, dose plans, reminders, and dose records in one place.

02

Health Connect

When enabled, Evolune can read body-weight data from Android Health Connect after explicit user authorization.

READ_WEIGHT only · foreground and user-controlled
03

Encrypted backups

Backups are encrypted on the device with a passphrase before they leave it, so the user remains responsible for the key to restoration.

AES-256-GCM · PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256
04

Wear OS companion

View medication information and record supported dose actions from a connected Wear OS device.

Optional, by design

Why Evolune requests Google Drive access

Google Drive access is optional and is used only when the user manually chooses to back up or restore Evolune data. Evolune uses Google’s appDataFolder, a private application-specific storage area. It does not browse, read, or modify unrelated files in the user’s Google Drive.
