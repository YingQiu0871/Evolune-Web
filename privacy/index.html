Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This policy describes what Evolune stores on your device, what it can access when you choose to connect Android Health Connect or Google Drive, and what remains under your control.

On this page Overview Data stored Health Connect Google Drive Encryption Sharing & deletion Contact

01

Overview

Evolune is a personal information and tracking tool for Android and Wear OS. It is designed around local storage and user-initiated data actions. Evolune does not operate a developer backend that stores users’ medication records, health data, or Google Drive backup contents.

02

Data stored by Evolune

Depending on the features you use, Evolune may store the following on your device:

  • Medication plans and dose schedules.
  • Dose records and related application records.
  • Body weight used for supported health-related calculations.
  • Supported settings, application preferences, and display choices.

This information is primarily stored locally. Evolune does not sell personal or health data and does not use Google user data for advertising.

03

Health Connect

Health Connect integration is optional and requires explicit user authorization. Evolune requests READ_WEIGHT only.

When the feature is enabled and authorized, Evolune may read the latest valid body-weight observation from the previous 30 days. Evolune does not write body weight to Health Connect, does not write or export medication records to Health Connect, and does not perform background Health Connect synchronization.

04

Google Drive backup and restore

Evolune uses Google Drive only for user-initiated backup and restore. Backups and restores are manually started by the user; Evolune does not continuously synchronize application data with Google Drive.

Evolune requests access only to Google Drive’s appDataFolder scope. This is an application-specific storage area. Evolune does not browse, read, or modify unrelated files in your Google Drive.

Evolune does not operate a developer backend that receives copies of users’ Google Drive backup contents.

05

Backup encryption

Backup data is encrypted on your device before upload. The current backup format uses:

Encryption
AES-256-GCM
Password-based key derivation
PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256
Default iterations
600,000

The backup passphrase is provided by you. Evolune does not have a service capable of recovering a forgotten backup passphrase. Losing the passphrase may make an encrypted backup unavailable for restoration.

06

Google OAuth data

Evolune uses Google’s authorization APIs to request the Drive appDataFolder permission. Evolune does not operate a backend that stores Google OAuth refresh tokens and does not request offline backend access.

Disconnecting Google from Evolune clears the current Evolune authorization session. It does not delete existing backup files and does not automatically revoke your Google account’s OAuth grant.

07

Data sharing

Evolune does not sell users’ personal or health data and does not use Google user data for advertising. Evolune does not operate a developer backend to receive copies of users’ Google Drive backup contents.

Android, Google Drive, Health Connect, and other infrastructure providers may process information according to their own products, policies, and permissions. Their services are not operated by Evolune.

08

Data retention and deletion

You may remove local Evolune data by using the application’s available data-management functions or by uninstalling the application.

Google Drive backups can be removed through Evolune’s supported backup-management functionality where available, or through applicable Google account and application-data controls. Disconnecting authorization alone does not delete existing backup files.

09

Network services

Evolune may use network access for user-initiated Google Drive backup and restore, Google authorization, and update information from GitHub Releases. Evolune does not send medication records or health data to an Evolune-operated server.

10

Security

Evolune is designed to keep application data local where possible and to encrypt backups before upload. No software or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your device, Google account, and backup passphrase protected.

11

Children

Evolune is not directed to children and is not intended to collect personal information from children.

12

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Evolune’s features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page identifies the current version.
