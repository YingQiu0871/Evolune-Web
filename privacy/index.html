Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This policy explains, in plain language, what Evolune stores on your device, what it can access when you choose to connect Android Health Connect or Google Drive, how backups are protected, and which decisions remain under your control.

On this page Overview Data stored Health Connect Google Drive Encryption Sharing & deletion Contact

01

Overview

Evolune is a personal information and tracking tool for Android and Wear OS. Its everyday design starts with local storage and with data actions that you deliberately initiate, rather than with a developer-operated account or data warehouse.

Evolune does not operate a developer backend that stores users’ medication records, health data, or copies of their Google Drive backup contents.

02

Data stored by Evolune

Depending on the features you choose to use, Evolune may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Medication plans, schedules, dose details, and reminders.
  • Dose records and other records you create inside the application.
  • Body-weight information used for supported health-related calculations.
  • Supported settings, application preferences, and display choices.

This information is primarily stored locally so that the application can provide its core features without requiring an Evolune-operated account. Evolune does not sell personal or health data, and it does not use Google user data for advertising.

03

Health Connect

Health Connect integration is optional and begins only after you explicitly grant permission. Evolune requests the READ_WEIGHT permission only.

When the feature is enabled and authorized, Evolune may read the latest valid body-weight observation from the previous 30 days to support the relevant in-app calculations. Evolune does not write body weight to Health Connect, does not write or export medication records to Health Connect, and does not perform background synchronization.

04

Google Drive backup and restore

Evolune uses Google Drive only when you manually choose to create a backup or restore one. Nothing in this feature requires continuous synchronization, and Evolune does not silently copy your application data to Google Drive in the background.

Evolune requests access only to Google Drive’s appDataFolder scope. This is a private, application-specific storage area intended for Evolune’s own files. Evolune does not browse, read, or modify unrelated files in your Google Drive.

Evolune does not operate a developer backend that receives or stores copies of your Google Drive backup contents.

05

Backup encryption

Backup data is encrypted on your device before it is uploaded. This means the backup is prepared locally first, and the current format uses the following:

Encryption
AES-256-GCM
Password-based key derivation
PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256
Default iterations
600,000

You provide the backup passphrase. Evolune does not have a service capable of recovering a forgotten passphrase, so please keep it somewhere safe and separate from the backup itself. Losing it may make an encrypted backup unavailable for restoration.

06

Google OAuth data

Evolune uses Google’s authorization APIs to request the Drive appDataFolder permission when you choose to use backup or restore. Evolune does not operate a backend that stores Google OAuth refresh tokens, and it does not request offline backend access for a server.

Disconnecting Google from Evolune clears the current authorization session held by the application. It does not delete existing backup files, and it does not automatically revoke the OAuth grant from your Google account; those actions are separate choices you can make through the relevant controls.

07

Data sharing

Evolune does not sell users’ personal or health data and does not use Google user data for advertising. It also does not operate a developer backend to receive copies of users’ Google Drive backup contents.

Android, Google Drive, Health Connect, and other infrastructure providers may process information according to their own products, privacy policies, and permission systems. Those services are operated independently and are not controlled by Evolune.

08

Data retention and deletion

You may remove local Evolune data by using the application’s available data-management functions or by uninstalling the application. The exact steps may vary slightly by Android version and device.

Google Drive backups can be removed through Evolune’s supported backup-management functionality where available, or through the applicable Google account and application-data controls. Disconnecting authorization alone does not delete existing backup files.

09

Network services

Evolune may use network access when you initiate a Google Drive backup or restore, complete Google authorization, or request update information from GitHub Releases. These connections support the feature you selected; Evolune does not send medication records or health data to an Evolune-operated server.

10

Security

Evolune is designed to keep application data local where possible and to encrypt backups before upload. Even so, no software, device, account, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keeping your device, Google account, and backup passphrase protected remains an important part of protecting your information.

11

Children

Evolune is not directed to children and is not intended to collect personal information from children.

12

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Evolune’s features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page identifies the current version, so you can quickly see whether the policy has been revised since your last visit.

13
